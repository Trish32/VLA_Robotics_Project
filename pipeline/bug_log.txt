Pipeline glue — bug log
========================
Scope: the handoffs between stages. RGB-D -> SLAM -> scene export -> OpenMask3D ->
FoundationPose -> ROS2. Every bug here is geometric and silent: nothing crashes, the
wrong thing is simply described with full confidence.


[1] open3d 0.20.0 ScalableTSDFVolume integrates nothing on arm64 macOS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Date:        2026-09-21
Fingerprint: `fuse_tsdf` returned an empty point cloud for input that is obviously
             valid. Narrowed with a direct probe:

               create_from_rgbd_image           -> 76800 points, mean z = 1.5  (correct)
               ScalableTSDFVolume.extract_point_cloud       -> 0
               ScalableTSDFVolume.extract_voxel_point_cloud -> 0
               UniformTSDFVolume.extract_voxel_point_cloud  -> 58525
               UniformTSDFVolume.extract_point_cloud        -> 11956

             `extract_voxel_point_cloud` returning 0 is what localised it: that reads
             raw voxels, so the failure is in `integrate()`, not in surface extraction
             or normal estimation.

Ruled out along the way (each was a plausible theory, each was wrong):
  - lattice alignment. The first hypothesis was that a plane at exactly z=1.5 with
    voxel_length=0.02 puts the zero-crossing precisely on voxel corners. Testing
    z=1.51 gave 0 as well.
  - degenerate single view. Three views with a 5 cm baseline: still 0.
  - colour type. NoColor with convert_rgb_to_intensity=True: still 0.

Cause:       ScalableTSDFVolume.integrate() is broken in open3d 0.20.0 on this platform.
             It does not raise — it returns normally having written nothing.

Fix:         `fuse_tsdf` uses UniformTSDFVolume, with bounds derived from the camera
             track padded by the depth horizon and resolution capped at 512 (allocation
             is O(resolution^3)).

Why this one matters out of proportion to its size: an empty cloud is not an obviously
broken value. OpenMask3D would read it as "the room contains nothing", propose no
instances, and the pipeline would report a clean run with no objects found. The failure
would have looked like a segmentation problem, three stages downstream of the actual bug.


[2] Open3D integrates with WORLD-TO-CAMERA; our poses are CAMERA-TO-WORLD
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Date:        2026-09-21

`volume.integrate(rgbd, intrinsic, extrinsic)` wants the extrinsic, i.e. world-to-camera.
`slam_server.py` deliberately returns camera-to-world, because that is what
`nav_msgs/Odometry` wants (where the camera IS in the map frame) and what
`droid.terminate()` produces.

Passing ours straight through fuses a scene mirrored about the origin, which still looks
like a plausible room. `fuse_tsdf` inverts, and
test_fusion_places_geometry_in_front_of_the_camera pins it: a camera at the origin
looking down +z at a 1.5 m wall must yield points near z = +1.5, not -1.5.

This is the same convention hazard as the DROID pose direction and the scalar-last
quaternion. Three different places in this pipeline where a sign or an inversion is
invisible in the output's shape.


NOTE — the gauge, and why SceneWriter refuses without it
----------------------------------------------------------
Monocular DROID normalises disparity (`depth_video.normalize`), so its translations
carry an arbitrary scale. Fusing those gives a scene whose size is wrong by an unknown
factor — and since the per-instance mesh cut from that scene is what FoundationPose
registers against, the 6-DoF pose and the pre-grasp offset inherit the error.

Nothing downstream can detect it: a room scaled by 0.7 is a perfectly consistent room.
So `SceneWriter` refuses a non-metric trajectory at the boundary rather than letting it
propagate, and `slam_server.py` reports `metric` so the boundary has something to check.
